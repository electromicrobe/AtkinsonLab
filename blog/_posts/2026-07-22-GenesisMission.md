The Atkinson lab was selected to lead one of the inaugural projects under the [U.S. Department of Energy (DOE)'s Genesis Mission: Transforming Science and Energy with AI](https://www.energy.gov/undersecretaryforscience/genesis-mission/genesis-mission).

Our project, MOBILE-PATH, will develop AI-driven approaches to understand and predict horizontal gene transfer in microbial communities.  

Out group is working with an outstanding multidisciplinary team of Princeton Engineering colleague [Jürgen Hackl](https://cee.princeton.edu/people/j%C3%BCrgen-hackl) who runs the [Complex Infrastructure Systems lab](https://cis.princeton.edu/) and our partner [Ilenne Del Valle Kessra](https://www.ornl.gov/staff/ilenne-d-del-valle-kessra/) at [Oak Ridge National Laboratory](https://www.ornl.gov/biosciences-division/synthetic-biology-section/) to advance AI-driven microbiome engineering and accelerate scientific discovery.
